Why everything runs on your Mac

Embedding generation, language-model inference, and the vector store all run on the Mac Mini. The Legal Intelligence app reads from a local SQLite metadata index. Document parsing happens in-process. No round trips to a cloud provider — for any data, ever.

Your data stays in the building

This is the architectural promise of paid Granid: no customer document, query, or embedding ever leaves your office. nDSG and Art. 321 StGB compliant by design.

We deliver and configure the Mac Mini as part of setup. After installation the device is fully under your control — sit it on a shelf, plug it into your LAN, and it runs.
