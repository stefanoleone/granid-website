Recommended hardware

Bring your own Mac.
We recommend the specs.

Hardware is not included in any tier. Granid runs on a Mac you own and keep in your office. Below: the specs we recommend so the software runs comfortably with all features enabled.

See pricing

Mac Mini (Essential, Professional)

Apple Silicon, 64 GB unified memory. Quiet, low power, fits on any desk.

Mac Studio (Enterprise)

Apple Silicon, 64 GB+ unified memory. Recommended when more than four professionals share the same instance.

macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer

Required to keep all of Granid's processing on the device.

Why 64 GB of unified memory

Paid tiers keep every part of Granid on your Mac: document search, language understanding, and your document index. 64 GB is the floor that lets all of it run alongside macOS, your browser, and document parsing without slowing down. Less and the system crawls; more is comfort, not requirement.

Your data stays in the building

Every step happens on your Mac: documents are imported, understood, indexed, and searched without ever contacting an outside service. No customer document, no question you ask, and no derived data ever leaves your office. nDSG and Art. 321 StGB compliant by design.

You acquire the Mac through your usual channel: Apple, an authorised reseller, or refurbished. We provision the license and send you the install package; you run it on the Mac.

Just want to try it first?

The 2-week trial runs on any Mac with Apple Silicon and 8 GB of RAM. During the trial, the heavy processing happens on a service hosted by Granid, so the hardware bar is deliberately low. Paid tiers run everything locally.
