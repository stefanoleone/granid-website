Hardware: Trial

Run Granid on your
existing Mac.

During the 2-week trial you don't need new hardware. Granid runs on any modern Apple Silicon Mac. Paid tiers recommend more capable specs. See the recommended hardware page.

Start your trial

Apple Silicon

M1, M2, M3 or M4. Intel Macs are not supported.

8 GB unified memory

Minimum. 16 GB is comfortable but not required for trial use.

macOS 14 or newer

Sonoma or later. Ventura and earlier are not supported.

Why a lightweight Mac is enough

During the trial, the Granid app on your Mac handles only document import and basic indexing. The heavier processing (understanding your documents and generating answers) runs on a remote service operated by Granid. This is what lets you evaluate the product without upgrading to the recommended paid-tier hardware.

Important: trial data leaves your office

During the 2-week trial, the documents and questions you submit are processed on a remote service operated by Granid. This means the data leaves your Mac during the trial. This is the opposite of the paid product, where nothing leaves your office. Please use only non-sensitive or anonymised data during the trial.

Paid tiers run fully local on a Mac (Mini or Studio, Apple Silicon, 64 GB+) that stays in your office. No customer data leaves the building.

Ready for production?

Paid tiers run on a Mac you own (Apple Silicon, 64 GB of unified memory). Everything runs on the device. Nothing leaves the building.
