Hardware — Trial

Run Granid on your
existing Mac.

During the 2-week trial you don't need new hardware. Granid runs on any modern Apple Silicon Mac. The Mac Mini M4 Pro is required only when you move to a paid tier.

Apple Silicon

M1, M2, M3 or M4. Intel Macs are not supported.

8 GB unified memory

Minimum. 16 GB is comfortable but not required for trial use.

macOS 14 or newer

Sonoma or later. Ventura and earlier are not supported.

Why a lightweight Mac is enough

During the trial, Granid keeps the local app thin. Document parsing and the SQLite metadata index run on your Mac. Embedding generation and language-model inference run on Granid-hosted remote services. This is what lets you evaluate the product without buying production hardware.

Important: trial data leaves your office

Because trial inference runs on Granid-hosted remote services, the documents and queries you submit during the 2-week trial are processed by those services. This inverts the core "no data leaves the office" promise of the paid product. Please use only non-sensitive or anonymized data during the trial.

Paid tiers run fully local on a Mac Mini M4 Pro installed in your office. No customer data leaves the building.

Ready for production?

Paid tiers run on a dedicated Mac Mini M4 Pro (64 GB) installed in your office. All inference and embedding stay local.
