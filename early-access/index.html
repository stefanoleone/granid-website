Early Access signup

Join the Early Access Program

Legal Intelligence, before general availability. Free for 2 months, on hardware you already own (Mac or Windows). Places are limited. Once you register and confirm your email, you receive the download links for the installer and a license valid for 2 months.

What to expect

  • Pre-production software. The core works; the polish is in progress. Expect rough edges and the occasional bug.
  • A direct line to us. Your feedback goes straight to the people building the product, and participants' feature proposals can make it into the product.
  • Two months, free. The Early Access license runs for 2 months: enough time to work with it, not just look at it.
  • A preferential price afterwards. Participants receive a discount on the Essential license when Legal Intelligence becomes commercially available.
  • One program per firm. Early Access replaces the regular trial for your firm. Think of it as a longer one.

What you need

  • Mac. Apple Silicon (M1 or later), 8 GB of RAM or more, 150–200 GB of free disk. The AI runs remotely by default; on Macs with enough memory you can choose to run it locally during onboarding.
  • Windows. Windows 10 or later, 8 GB of RAM or more, 150–200 GB of free disk. The AI runs remotely.
  • Patience on day one. Legal Intelligence downloads the complete Swiss legal corpus on first run. Working with your own documents starts immediately; legal research is ready once the download completes.
