The problem with cloud AI

Every time you use cloud AI tools with client documents, you risk violating attorney-client privilege under Art. 321 StGB and the revised Federal Act on Data Protection (nFADP/nDSG, in force since 1 September 2023). Your clients trust you with their most sensitive information. That trust should not travel to external servers.
