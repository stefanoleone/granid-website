Voice Intelligence: on-device dictation that never uploads

12 May 2026 Granid 1 min read
Voice Intelligence On-premise AI

Dictation is where confidential matters are spoken aloud first. That is exactly the moment cloud transcription should not be in the room.

Most dictation tools stream your audio to a server for transcription. For a lawyer describing a client matter, that is the same privilege problem as cloud AI, just through a microphone instead of a document upload.

On the device, by default

Voice Intelligence by Granid runs speech recognition on your Apple Silicon Mac. The audio is transcribed locally, cleaned up, and structured without ever leaving the Mac. It learns your personal vocabulary (party names, Latin terms, statute abbreviations), so the output needs less correction over time.

  • No audio leaves the Mac.
  • A personal vocabulary that improves with use.
  • Available from the Professional tier upward, integrated into Legal Intelligence.

The most private place to speak about a case is a device that cannot send the recording anywhere.

Granid runs entirely on a Mac in your office. Your client data never leaves the building.
