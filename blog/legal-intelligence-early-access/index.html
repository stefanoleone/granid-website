Legal Intelligence Early Access is now open

9 July 2026 Granid 3 min read
Early Access

We are opening Early Access to Legal Intelligence, the AI built for Swiss law firms. In this pre-production phase we are looking for a small group of lawyers, legal professionals, and AI specialists to put it to work and help decide what we build next.

What Legal Intelligence is

Legal Intelligence is an AI assistant for Swiss legal work. It researches across Swiss federal legislation, court decisions, and legal commentary in German, French, and Italian, and it answers with citations you can check: every citation is verified against its source rather than taken on trust. In production it runs entirely on hardware the firm owns, so client data never leaves the office.

Why we are opening Early Access

A legal AI is only as valuable as the trust of the professionals who rely on it. Before general availability, we want candid, real-world feedback from the two groups who will judge it hardest:

  • Lawyers and legal professionals: is it accurate, is it fast, and does it fit the way you actually work?
  • AI specialists: how do our retrieval, ranking, and citation-verification choices hold up under scrutiny?

What you tell us in this phase directly shapes what ships.

How Early Access works (please read)

So you can evaluate the product without setting up dedicated hardware, during Early Access the AI models run remotely rather than on your own machine.

Because of that, one rule comes before all others: please do not use real or confidential client documents during Early Access. Use only fictitious, non-sensitive, or fully anonymized material. This constraint is specific to the evaluation phase. The commercial product runs fully on-premise, with no data leaving your office; that is the core promise of Legal Intelligence, and Early Access is the one phase where it does not yet apply.

Early Access runs as a macOS application on Apple Silicon, and because the AI runs remotely during the program, you do not need the production hardware: any Mac with Apple Silicon is enough to take part. If you work on Windows, tell us all the same. Should enough legal professionals on Windows register their interest, we may offer a Windows trial as well, so you can evaluate Legal Intelligence before investing in a Mac and a license. That is exactly why we ask which machine you use when you write to us: your answer tells us whether to build it.

What comes after Early Access

When the program concludes, Legal Intelligence becomes commercially available. A trial option will let you evaluate it using remote models identical to the ones the product runs locally, followed by three tiers: Essential, Professional, and Enterprise. We are starting in Switzerland, with France, Germany, and Italy to follow.

How to take part

Places in the program are limited. If you would like to help shape Legal Intelligence, write to us and we will be in touch.
