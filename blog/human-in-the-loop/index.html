Human in the Loop: why AI assists and lawyers decide

28 April 2026 Granid 1 min read
Legal Intelligence

"Human in the Loop" is not a disclaimer. It is a design constraint that shapes every part of how Granid presents its work.

A legal answer carries professional responsibility. That responsibility cannot be delegated to a model, and Granid is built so that it never is.

Assistance, not automation

Granid surfaces relevant passages, extracts deadlines, and drafts. Every output is traceable to its source and presented for review, not for blind acceptance. The lawyer stays the decision-maker at every step.

  • Every recommendation links back to the material it rests on.
  • Nothing is hidden behind a confidence score you cannot inspect.
  • The system is transparent about what it does not know.

The goal is a lawyer who is faster and more certain. Not one who has stopped checking.

This is why verified citations, local processing, and human review are not separate features. They are the same principle: the technology serves the professional's judgment, and never substitutes for it.

Granid runs entirely on a Mac in your office. Your client data never leaves the building.
