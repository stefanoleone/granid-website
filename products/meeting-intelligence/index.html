Meeting Intelligence by Granid Coming soon

Your meetings, understood locally.

Meeting Intelligence brings multilingual transcripts, structured notes, and decision tracking to legal meetings, all running on the Mac you own. Audio and notes never leave the firm.

Contact us for early access
Multilingual transcripts

German, French, Italian, English. The same legal vocabulary that powers Legal Intelligence, applied to spoken word.

Structured notes

Decisions, action items, and open questions extracted from the transcript and linked back to the matter file.

Fully on-premise

Audio, transcripts, and notes are processed and stored on your Mac. Nothing reaches a third-party meeting service.

Why local meeting AI

Cloud meeting tools record every word a firm says about its clients. Granting that recording to an external vendor is incompatible with attorney-client privilege under Art. 321 StGB. Meeting Intelligence is built so the recording never exists outside your office.
