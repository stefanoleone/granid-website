Why local dictation

Cloud dictation services route the most sensitive content a Swiss lawyer produces, the spoken word, through external servers. That route is incompatible with attorney-client privilege under Art. 321 StGB and with the nDSG. Voice Intelligence keeps the audio, the model, and the transcript on the Mac you already use for Legal Intelligence.
