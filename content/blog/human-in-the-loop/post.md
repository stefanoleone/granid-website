A legal answer carries professional responsibility. That responsibility cannot
be delegated to a model, and Granid is built so that it never is.

## Assistance, not automation

Granid surfaces relevant passages, extracts deadlines, and drafts. Every
output is traceable to its source and presented for review, not for blind
acceptance. The lawyer stays the decision-maker at every step.

- Every recommendation links back to the material it rests on.
- Nothing is hidden behind a confidence score you cannot inspect.
- The system is transparent about what it does not know.

> The goal is a lawyer who is faster and more certain. Not one who has stopped checking.

This is why verified citations, local processing, and human review are not
separate features. They are the same principle: the technology serves the
professional's judgment, and never substitutes for it.
