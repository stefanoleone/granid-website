Most dictation tools stream your audio to a server for transcription. For a
lawyer describing a client matter, that is the same privilege problem as cloud
AI, just through a microphone instead of a document upload.

## On the device, by default

**Voice Intelligence by Granid** runs speech recognition on your Apple Silicon
Mac. The audio is transcribed locally, cleaned up, and structured without ever
leaving the Mac. It learns your personal vocabulary (party names, Latin terms,
statute abbreviations), so the output needs less correction over time.

- No audio leaves the Mac.
- A personal vocabulary that improves with use.
- Available from the **Professional** tier upward, integrated into Legal Intelligence.

> The most private place to speak about a case is a device that cannot send the recording anywhere.
